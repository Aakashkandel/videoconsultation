Why is SharedArrayBuffer important?

For Zoom Video SDK, SharedArrayBuffer enables:

  • ✅ 720p WebAssembly video (high quality)
  • ✅ Background noise suppression
  • ✅ Virtual backgrounds (Firefox/Safari)
  • ✅ Better performance and advanced features

Required HTTP Headers:

  • Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy: same-origin
  • Cross-Origin-Embedder-Policy: require-corp

Note: These headers must be set on the server. If SharedArrayBuffer is not available, the Zoom Video SDK will fall back to WebRTC video or lower quality modes.
